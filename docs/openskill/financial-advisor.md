# Financial advisor

- **Id**: https://logicsrc.com/openskill/financial-advisor
- **Kind**: occupation
- **Domain**: Finance
- **Language**: en
- **Aliases**: financial adviser, personal financial advisor

A role that helps clients understand their financial position and plan toward their financial objectives.

## Includes

- Considering goals, resources, uncertainty and competing financial priorities.
- Explaining recommendations and the assumptions and constraints behind them.
