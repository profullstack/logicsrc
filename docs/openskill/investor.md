# Investor

- **Id**: https://logicsrc.com/openskill/investor
- **Kind**: occupation
- **Domain**: Finance
- **Language**: en
- **Aliases**: capital investor

A role that commits and manages capital in assets or ventures with an expectation of future value.

## Includes

- Evaluating opportunities, deciding allocations and monitoring holdings.
- The role may be personal or professional; the label alone does not establish employment or an advisory service.
